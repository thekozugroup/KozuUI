## Design contract

Use `Kozu Universal UI Kit.figcraft` as the editable design file, `Kozu.tokens.json` as the portable contract and `web/kozu.css` for web implementation. Page 02 defines materials and typography. Pages 03–05 cover Accounts, Tasks and Activity. Page 06 defines activity and progress indicators, page 07 contains the component catalog, page 08 covers platform adaptations, and page 09 contains the icon library. Component specimen pages follow page 09. Use `docs/foundations.md`, `docs/platforms.md`, `docs/patterns.md` and `docs/components.md` for implementation rules. Inspect interactive specimens in `web/guide.html`.

- Use Kozu as the sole product wordmark. Accounts, Tasks and Activity are destinations in one system. Use blue-to-jade materials throughout, with dark navy and white surfaces as the context requires. Use Inter for body, controls and finances, with 17 units preferred for prose. Use Helvetica Neue for compact display and voice headings, with Inter or system sans as fallback. Use Menlo for telemetry and the system face for native chrome.
- Use Orange, Green, Blue, Gold, Violet and Rose hue ramps and one Graphite neutral ramp. Expose 11 steps from 50 through 950 and shared aliases across adapters. Use Blue for general action and information, Orange for attention, Green for recovery, and Gold, Violet and Rose for comparative series.
- Keep background fields static. Use the bundled images in `Assets/Materials` and portraits in `Assets/People`. Reuse semantic variables and component instances. On import, check variable definitions and remap imported IDs against the destination document.
- In Accounts, use tabular figures for aligned balances. Keep the fractional amount legible and on the same baseline. Expose the complete localized currency value to assistive technology. Use Morgan Lee and Sam Rivera in sample transactions. Show both amounts on the goal bar and size the fill by the saved amount divided by the target.
- In Tasks, use Inbox, Flow and Scout as the agent names and Detailed as the setting label. Preserve readable text on translucent panels. Pair the listening state with a visible label and a Stop control. Keep the waveform steady under reduced motion. Use two rows for progress, three for compact activity and up to seven for a wide peak waveform. Follow the fixed-pitch dot matrix spec in `docs/foundations.md`. Fill `floor(totalVisibleDots * percent / 100)` whole dots column by column from leading to trailing, top then bottom. At 0% all dots are dim. At 100% all dots are lit. Display and expose the exact progress percentage separately.
- In Activity, pair orange alerts, blue investigation events and green recovery events with a glyph and a status word. Keep command, evidence and outcome in reading order.

## Controls and layout

- Use fill, opacity, spacing and type weight to separate surfaces and show state. Keep surfaces and controls borderless. Preserve icon strokes, data marks, selection checks and functional console separators. Use 28-unit blur on frosted controls, cards and toolbars, and 40-unit blur on overlays. Apply `#FCFCFC` or `#0D1726` with the light or dark fill opacities in `docs/foundations.md`. Verify contrast on the brightest and darkest crops. Use opaque fills for reduced transparency and dense reading.
- Use Lucide from Aria Icons on a 24-unit grid with a 1.8-unit source stroke. Preserve its license. In text buttons, match the icon’s visible height to the label’s cap height. Center the icon in the rounded endcap and center the label in the full button with balanced slots. Regular touch targets are at least 48 × 48 units. The compact pointer minimum is 32 × 32 units. Use an 8-unit sibling gap.
- Derive adjacent nested radii with `max(0, outerRadius - inset)`. Center chip contents, keep both ends of floating bars balanced and leave room for visible focus and text. Show focus with tone and text emphasis independently of selection. Preserve keyboard and remote input and respect reduced motion.
- Use compact navigation below 600 units. At 600, introduce a vertically centered 72-unit rail 16 units from the leading safe edge, with 8-unit padding, 56-unit targets and 8-unit gaps. Introduce two panes from 720 when they fit and a sidebar from 840. Preserve selection, scroll and input when the viewport changes.
- Respect native safe areas, widget bounds, text scaling, wearable targets and TV focus navigation. Keep wearable layouts safe within the host’s round or rectangular bounds. Use tonal surfaces on Wear OS and tonal or host-provided widget backgrounds as appropriate.

## Verification

After changing the native kit, run `python3 -B tools/verify_geometry.py --self-test`, `python3 -B tools/verify_geometry.py` and `python3 -B tools/verify_system.py`. Update affected exports, tokens, inventories and web implementation together. Test the web entry with `node web/check.mjs /path/to/task-scratch`. On each shipping platform, verify accessible names, focus return, keyboard traversal and blur performance. Test at least 200% text scaling, with a 140% watch baseline and the native maximum where available.
